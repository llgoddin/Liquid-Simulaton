# SPH solver formulation

`Particle_Manager` uses a two-dimensional weakly-compressible SPH solver when its existing Repulse toggle is enabled. The 2D poly6 density kernel, spiky pressure gradient, and nonnegative 2D viscosity Laplacian are normalized over a disk of support radius `h`; density includes self contribution. The linear equation of state is `p=max(0,k*(rho-rho0))`, an explicit free-surface negative-pressure clamp.

Pressure uses the symmetric Becker–Teschner conservative pair-force equation `-mi*mj*(pi/rhoi² + pj/rhoj²)*grad(W)`; viscosity uses `mu*mi*mj/(rhoi*rhoj)*(vj-vi)*L`. These are forces, accumulated equal and opposite, not Müller force-density equations or Becker acceleration equations. The 2D kernels follow David Bindel, *Deriving SPH*, §§2–3 (https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~bindel/class/cs5220-f11/code/sph-derive.pdf); the symmetric pressure form follows Becker and Teschner, *Weakly compressible SPH for free surface flows*, §3 (https://cg.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/publications/2007_SCA_SPH.pdf). At distinct coincident positions the undefined pressure direction is zero; viscosity remains active.

The solver computes densities, pressures and pair forces from one pre-move position/velocity snapshot. The manager converts physical SPH force by 1/120 for compatibility with `Particle.move`'s legacy 120 Hz acceleration multiplier. Gravity, mouse force, fixed stepping, bounds and obstacle handling remain in their existing paths. This compatibility conversion is not a full units migration. Manager safety guards cap the legacy force magnitude at 50, speed at 500 px/s, and a direct physics step at 1/120 s; negative or non-finite durations become zero. These broad bounds are runaway guards, not SPH calibration or stability criteria. Boundary density correction, validated compressibility limits and performance qualification remain out of scope; the chosen defaults are not a stability guarantee.
